# Valknut Brand Intelligence v1.4.0 — Enterprise Brand Protection Suite

## 🌎 Mercado Libre Latin America Ecosystem Integration
- **Multi-Country Direct Harvesting**: Full support for single-region and Latin America multi-sweeps across **Mexico (MLM)**, **Brazil (MLB)**, **Argentina (MLA)**, **Colombia (MCO)**, **Chile (MLC)**, and **Peru (MPE)**.
- **Deep Organic Scan Depth**: Integrated 1 Page (50), 2 Pages (100), 5 Pages (250), and 10 Pages (500) pagination presets.
- **Deep Catalog & Buy Box Disentanglement**: Advanced catalog parsing extracts Buy Box winners, specific winner IDs (wid=), and competing catalog merchants from /p/ product pages.
- **Real-Time Storefront & Origin Enrichment**: Multi-line DOM parser with active React hydration polling extracts verified seller nicknames, shipping origin (e.g. USA, Mexico), and MercadoLíder Platinum badges.
- **On-Demand Visual Syndicate & Connected Seller Hunter**: Discovers connected network assets across:
  - Publicaciones del vendedor (Seller Storefront Syndicates)
  - Quienes vieron este producto también compraron (Competitors & Cross-Listings)
  - Otras opciones de compra (Competing Buy Box Sellers)
- **Cloudflare & Anti-Bot Evasion**: Offscreen human browser execution avoiding captcha walls and lock collisions.
